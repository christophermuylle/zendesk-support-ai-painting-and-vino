# Phoenix, AZ

Status: OPEN for private events as of 2026-10-02 (confirmed by Christopher).

Until 2026-10-02 this file said "coming soon - not yet operating", which is
what the AI told anyone who asked. That is no longer true for private events.

## Private events
Available for birthdays, bachelorette parties, corporate team-building,
fundraisers and kids' parties, on the same terms as the other launched
locations - see shared.md's Private events section for the full pricing table
and reply structure.

Minimum group size: 8 guests (Christopher, 2026-10-02).

Per-person rates are shared.md's default table - Standard 45/40/35,
Corporate 50/45/40 - the same group as San Diego, Orange County, Los Angeles,
Sacramento, Riverside County and Tucson.

Area covered: Phoenix, Scottsdale, Glendale, Tolleson, Union Hills, Fountain
Hills, Peoria, the Biltmore district, the Arcadia district, Tempe, Chandler,
Gilbert and Maricopa (Christopher, 2026-10-02).
