# Orange County, CA

Source: paintingandvino.com/orange-county - confirm current before relying
on it.

## Pricing
Most standard classes: $45. Specialty events (e.g. "Paint your pet"): $55.
Drinks-included options: $60.

## Booking
Book through the event listing's "Get Tickets" button - each event has its
own registration page. Seating is limited per class, so booking in advance
is recommended. Exact venue/address is always listed on the event page.

## Venues (examples - always confirm the specific one on the event listing)
Various partner locations from Anaheim to Huntington Beach and Newport
Beach.

## Private events
Available for birthdays and other celebrations - direct the customer to
"request a quote." No fixed minimum group size found on the location page.

## What's included
All art supplies and guided instruction. Food/drinks available for
purchase at the venue but not included.
