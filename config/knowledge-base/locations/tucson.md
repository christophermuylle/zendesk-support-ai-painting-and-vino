## Pricing
Most standard classes: $45. Range seen up to $270 for special/partner
events (e.g. a UnitedHealthcare Medicare private event was listed at
$160).

## Booking
Direct link: https://paintingandvino.com/tucson-paint-and-sip/
Book through the event listing's "Get Tickets" button - each event has its
own registration page. Seating is limited per class, so booking in advance
is recommended.

## Venues (examples - always confirm the specific one on the event listing)

## Private events
Available for birthdays, bachelorette celebrations, and corporate
gatherings - direct the customer to request a quote. No fixed minimum
group size found on the location page.

## What's included
All art supplies and guided instruction. Food/drinks available for
purchase at the venue but not included.
