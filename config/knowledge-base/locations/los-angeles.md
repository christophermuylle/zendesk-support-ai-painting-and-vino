# Los Angeles, CA

Source: paintingandvino.com/los-angeles - confirm current before relying
on it.

## Pricing
Most standard classes: $45. Specialty events (e.g. "Paint your pet"):
around $55. Range seen: $45-$90. Private events by quote.

## Booking
Direct link: https://paintingandvino.com/los-angeles-paint-and-sip/
Book through the event listing's "Get Tickets" button - each event has its
own registration page. Seating is limited per class, so booking in advance
is recommended.

## Venues (examples - always confirm the specific one on the event listing)
Old Spaghetti Factory (Newport Beach), Mt. Lowe Brewing Co. (Arcadia),
Viceroy Hotel (Santa Monica), Green Cheek Beer Co. (Orange, Sunset Beach).
