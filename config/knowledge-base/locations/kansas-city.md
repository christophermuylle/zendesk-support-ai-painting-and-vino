# Kansas City, MO

Source: paintingandvino.com/kansas-city-paint-and-sip/ - confirm current
before relying on it.

## Pricing
Standard in-person classes: $35/ticket.

## Booking
Direct link: https://paintingandvino.com/kansas-city-paint-and-sip/
Book through the event listing's "Get Tickets" button - each event has its
own registration page. Seating is limited per class, so booking in advance
is recommended.

## Venues (examples - always confirm the specific one on the event listing)
OverBoard Coffee (Lee's Summit), Anchor Island Coffee (Kansas City), Hemma
Hemma (Kansas City), Rustic Retreat (Kearney), Lilly's Cantina (Kansas
City).

## Private events
Good for birthdays, bachelorette parties, bridal showers, and corporate
team-building. Minimum group size: 8 guests. Kansas City runs its own
lower pricing tier (see shared.md's Private events section for the full
table and reply structure).

Venue list (send when the customer needs a venue):

## What's included
All art supplies and guided instruction. Food/drinks available for
purchase at the venue but not included.
