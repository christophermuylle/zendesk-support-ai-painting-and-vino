# San Diego, CA

Source: paintingandvino.com/san-diego-county - confirm current before
relying on it.

## Pricing
Range: $45-$100 depending on the specific event/venue - check the
individual event listing for exact price.

## Booking
Book through the event listing's "Get Tickets" button - each event has its
own registration page. Seating is limited per class, so booking in advance
is recommended.

## Venues (examples - always confirm the specific one on the event listing)
Coastal to inland San Diego County - e.g. Beaumont's Eatery (La Jolla),
Pitfire Pizza (Carlsbad), DoubleTree (Del Mar).

## Private events
Available for birthdays, bachelorette parties, bridal showers, and
corporate team-building - direct the customer to "request a quote." No
fixed minimum group size found on the location page.

## What's included
All art supplies and professional instruction. Food/drinks available for
purchase at the venue but not included.
