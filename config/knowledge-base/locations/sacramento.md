# Sacramento, CA

Source: paintingandvino.com/sacramento - confirm current before relying on
it.

## Pricing
Standard events: $45/person.

## Booking
Direct link: https://paintingandvino.com/sacramento-paint-and-sip/
Book through the event listing's "Get Tickets" button - each event has its
own registration page. Seating is limited per class, so booking in advance
is recommended.

## Venues (examples - always confirm the specific one on the event listing)
Cross Roads Pizza and Pub (Rocklin), Brick & Barrel Kitchen & Bar
(Lincoln), La Popular (Roseville), The Stag Bar (Woodland), Back Forty
Texas BBQ (Roseville).

## Private events
Available for birthdays, bachelorette parties, etc. Minimum group size:
**10 guests** (higher than the 8-person default; see shared.md's Private
events section for the full pricing table and reply structure).

Venue list (send when the customer needs a venue):
https://drive.google.com/file/d/150NGoWBPzLopJ89S0SQKpOWkFEisYixb/view?usp=sharing

## What's included
All art supplies and guided instruction. Food/drinks available for
purchase at the venue but not included.
