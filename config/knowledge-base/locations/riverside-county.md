# Riverside County, CA (including Temecula)

Source: paintingandvino.com/riverside-county - confirm current before
relying on it.

## Pricing
No specific price range published on the page - do not guess a number;
tell the customer you'll confirm pricing, or point them to the event
listings on the site.

## Booking
Book through the event listing's "Get Tickets" button - each event has its
own registration page. Seating is limited per class, so booking in advance
is recommended.

## Venues
Classes run at local venue partners across the county, from the city of
Riverside out to Temecula wine country. The exact venue is always listed
on the specific event.

## Private events
Available for birthdays, bachelorette parties, bridal showers, and
corporate team-building - direct the customer to "request a quote." No
fixed minimum group size found on the location page.

## What's included
Canvas, paints, brushes, and an easel-side apron. Food/drinks available
for purchase at the venue but not included.
